      Content filter

      Comma-separated words matched against group names, like “US | News”, “USA: Sports” or “EN - Action”.

      The same station often appears in several folders and as HD, FHD, SD and RAW copies. Only the best copy is listed; the others are tried automatically if it won't play. A channel you've starred is always the one kept.

      Local stations

      Movies and Shows on the TV

      The TV shows one list of every title in these folders instead of the provider’s folder tree. A * at the end matches anything after (“VOD | EN*” takes “VOD | EN Action”, “VOD | EN Comedy”…). The rest of each folder name becomes a genre filter, and a folder like “IMDB TOP 500” becomes a collection. Leave empty to use every folder.

      Providers list the local station of every city under one folder (hundreds under “ABC”). With your city here, only yours are kept. Stations that name no city are kept. Channels are tidied once when a playlist loads.
